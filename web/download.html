After the download

Then what happens

1

Open it

Double-click what you downloaded. If your computer asks whether you’re sure, that’s normal for small independent apps — signing them properly would cost more than 359€ a year. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

2

Type a name

Three to sixteen characters. There is no account, no email and no password to come up with.

3

Press play

Pick a Minecraft version and go. The very first launch downloads the game, so give it a minute or two.

Nothing hidden in the download

Every button up there tells you how big the file is before you click it. No extra software rides along, no toolbars, no trial that expires — and nothing to pay, now or later.
