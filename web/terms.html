Terms

The rules, in plain language: use Cubeon, be decent to other players, and know that it's a fan project rather than a corporation.

Last updated 20 September 2026

The gist

Cubeon is a free, unofficial Minecraft launcher made by a fan. Play with it as much as you like. Don't use it to hurt, trick or impersonate anybody.

Playing fair

  • Don't pretend to be another player - names are protected precisely so this stays true.
  • Don't use the friends features to harass, threaten or spam anyone.
  • Don't try to break, overload or tamper with other people's names, skins or worlds.
  • Don't upload anything you don't have the right to use.

If someone breaks these rules, we can block that name or that copy of the app.

Your creations

Skins and pictures you upload stay yours. By uploading one, you give us permission to show it to other Cubeon players - which is the entire point of a skin.

Minecraft isn't ours

Cubeon is not made by, approved by or connected to Mojang or Microsoft. Minecraft is a trademark of Mojang AB, and you still need your own copy of the game as usual.

No promises

We build Cubeon carefully, but we can't promise it will work perfectly every time or that a world will never be lost. Keep a backup of anything you'd hate to lose.

Stopping

You can stop using Cubeon whenever you want, and delete your data as described on the privacy page. We can stop offering the app, or block someone who misuses it.
