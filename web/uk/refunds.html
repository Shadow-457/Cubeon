Refunds

There's nothing to refund right now, because Cubeon doesn't take money anywhere - not in the app, not on this site.

Last updated 20 September 2026

Nothing to buy

Downloading and using Cubeon is free, with every feature included. We never ask for card details, there are no subscriptions, no free trials that quietly start charging you, and no adverts to pay to remove.

If that ever changes

If we add something optional you can pay for one day, this page will say - before you pay - exactly what it costs, what you get, and how to get your money back. Our rule for that: at least 14 days, no questions asked, and a cancel button you can press yourself.

What we won't do

  • Charges you didn't agree to, or renewals you didn't ask for.
  • Fees that appear at the last step of a checkout.
  • Charging for anything that's needed to play with your friends.

Been charged by something?

If you ever see a charge connected to Cubeon, tell us straight away at hamza.457.shahbaz@gmail.com and we'll sort it out or point you to whoever can.
