Make it yours

  1. Open Cosmetics → Store in Cubeon and download an item.
  2. Choose it in Cape, Pets, Back, Head or Feet to equip it.
  3. Launch a supported Fabric or Quilt instance. Restart Minecraft after equipping a newly downloaded model.
Who can see my cosmetics?

Downloaded wings, pets and wearables appear on your character in your own game. They are currently local; other players do not automatically receive these models. Read about skins and shared capes.

Do glow and animations need shaders?

Gentle wing motion, hovering companions and luminous details work with Cubeon Client. Soft bloom around glowing details depends on your shaders. You can turn cosmetic motion off in the client settings.
